# Clear visible risk stays in the editor-facing CSV

The MVP keeps Scouting Candidates with clear Visible Risk in the editor-facing weekly CSV, marked with risk status, category, comment, and a lower priority label, unless another true exclusion reason applies. This deliberately differs from Found in Russian candidates, which are excluded from the CSV, because AutoScout is an editorial-intelligence tool rather than a legal decision system; visible risk should be preserved for human review instead of hidden by automation.
